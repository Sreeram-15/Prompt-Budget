# GTM

The exact target user is a technical founder, VP Engineering, or Head of Engineering at a 5-80 person AI-enabled SaaS startup that recently added multiple coding assistants, chat subscriptions, and direct model APIs. They feel the bill creeping up but do not yet have a finance owner who understands tool-level AI usage.

Right before wanting this tool, they are searching or scrolling for terms like "Cursor business pricing," "Claude team vs pro," "OpenAI API bill too high," "GitHub Copilot business worth it," or complaint threads about AI tool spend. They are likely in Hacker News comments, r/SaaS, r/startups, r/LocalLLaMA, Indie Hackers, Latent Space, AI Engineer Discord, founder Slack groups, and X lists around AI engineers and seed-stage founders.

The first 100 users with $0 paid budget would come from direct, useful distribution: post anonymized teardown threads of common AI spend stacks, comment on relevant pricing complaint threads with a free audit link, DM founders who recently posted hiring or AI product launches, ask VC platform teams to share it with portfolio operators, and publish one "AI stack receipt teardown" per day for a week.

The unfair channel for Credex is proprietary supply knowledge. Credex can say which categories of spend are actually eligible for discounted credits, which a generic calculator cannot know. That makes the audit feel less like content marketing and more like a live purchasing edge.

Week-1 traction if it works: 250 unique visitors, 90 completed audits, 30 email captures, 8 high-savings consultation requests, and at least 3 calls where the user brings a real invoice or vendor renewal question.
