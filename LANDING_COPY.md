## FAQ

**Do I need to connect billing accounts?**  
No. The MVP uses manually entered tools, seats, plans, and monthly spend.

**Do I have to enter email first?**  
No. The audit result appears before email capture.

**Is this financial advice?**  
No. It is a spend audit based on public pricing and user-provided spend.

**Why does Credex appear in some results?**  
Credex is relevant when savings are large enough that discounted AI infrastructure credits may be worth evaluating.

**Can I share the report publicly?**  
Yes. The public URL strips email, company name, and role.
