# Metrics

North Star metric: high-savings audits that book a Credex consultation. This matches the job of the product: deliver value first, then create qualified Credex demand.

Input metrics:

- Audit completion rate from landing page visit.
- Email capture rate after results.
- Percentage of audits with more than $500/month in savings.

Instrument first:

- Landing visit, form started, audit completed, savings bucket, email captured, public report opened, consultation CTA clicked.

Pivot trigger:

If 500 completed audits produce fewer than 10 consultation requests or fewer than 2 credible purchasing conversations, the product is attracting curiosity rather than buying intent and should pivot toward invoice-backed audits or a narrower ICP.
