# Economics

Assume a converted Credex customer buys $30,000 in discounted AI credits annually and Credex earns a 12% gross margin. That makes gross profit about $3,600 per converted customer before support and sales costs.

Channel CAC from the GTM plan should be low but not zero. Founder DMs may cost 20 minutes each; at an internal value of $40/hour, 30 DMs cost about $400 in labor. If 6 complete audits, 2 book calls, and 1 buys credits, CAC is $400. Community posts may take 6 hours of writing and replies, or $240 of labor; if one post drives 40 audits, 10 leads, 2 calls, and 0.5 expected purchases, CAC is about $480.

For profitability, the funnel can be modest. If 100 audits produce 25 emails, 6 consultations, and 1 credit purchase, gross profit is roughly $3,600. Even with $500-$1,000 of labor CAC, that works. The key conversion is audit completed to consultation booked; below 3%, the product is probably too broad or not surfacing enough pain.

To drive $1M ARR in 18 months, Credex would need roughly $83k/month in recognized annualized margin or enough credit volume to support that ARR target. At $3,600 gross profit per customer per year, that implies around 278 active converted customers. If audit-to-purchase conversion is 1%, the tool needs about 27,800 completed audits over 18 months, or about 1,545 per month. That is achievable only if distribution moves beyond manual posts into partner channels, investor newsletters, and shareable benchmark reports.
